The journey of one book

From the shelf, into your hands, and back again.

Follow a single copy as it moves through the library. Scroll and the book travels with you.

Shelved

It starts on a shelf.

Every copy has its own barcode, shelf location and call number, so staff always know where a book belongs and whether it is in.

Found

A student finds it.

Search by title, author, subject or ISBN. See whether a copy is on the shelf or when it is due back. If it is out, reserve it and wait your turn.

Borrowed

It goes home with you.

At the desk, one scan of your card and one scan of the book. The due date is set for you, and renewing later takes a single tap.

Returned

It comes back, and the next reader is told.

Returns are scanned in seconds. If a book is late, the fine shows the days and the rate. If someone reserved it, it moves straight to the hold shelf.

Safe by design

Sign in with a touch, not a password you forget.

Use a passkey with your fingerprint, face or device PIN. Prefer a password? That works too, and it is stored the careful way.

  • Passkeys and biometrics. Your fingerprint never leaves your device. The library only keeps a public key.
  • Strong passwords. Hashed with scrypt and a unique salt. Five wrong tries lock the account for fifteen minutes.
  • Hardened pages. Strict content policy, same-site cookies, request tokens against forged actions, and rate limits.
  • Every action logged. Admins can read who checked out, changed a setting or reset an account, and when.
  • Two databases, each doing what it does best. Books, copies and loans sit in SQL so counts always add up. Accounts, holds, fines and logs sit in a NoSQL store that stays flexible.
